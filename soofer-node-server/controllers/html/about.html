Welcome to Sooffer, where safety meets convenience in ridesharing.

Established in 2021, Sooffer was born out of a desire to redefine the rideshare landscape. Launching our services in Texas and Louisiana, we're not just another transportation service. We are a community dedicated to enhancing your travel experiences by ensuring reliability, safety, and comfort.

We believe that everyone deserves a secure and smooth ride. With this belief, we've invested in advanced technologies like biometric driver verification and One-Time Password (OTP) secured rides. We have also instituted an audio and video recording policy for all trips, prioritizing your safety above everything else.

We're also passionate about fair pricing. We offer standard pricing that allows passengers to know their costs upfront, with a minimal surge for high-demand periods.

For our drivers, we are committed to creating an environment that respects their work and pays fairly. As independent contractors under the U.S. 1099 tax regulations, our drivers enjoy true independence and better earnings.

At Sooffer, our vision is not confined within city limits. While we're starting in the heart of the United States, our sights are set on becoming a global service provider, reaching out to customers across continents, from Australia and Romania to Kenya.

Whether you're a passenger looking for a safe and fair-priced ride or a driver aspiring to work with a company that truly values your service, Sooffer is your destination.

Join us today and experience the Sooffer difference. Our journey has just begun, and we're excited to have you aboard. Welcome to a new era of ridesharing!
