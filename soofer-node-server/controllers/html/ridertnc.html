SOOFFER Driver Tracking System

How Sooffer driver tracking works and how we plan to use GPS data for autonomous driving.

The ride-sharing service Sooffer has always focused on riding the cutting edge of technology to stay ahead of its incumbent predecessors and competition. Sooffer driver tracking GPS on drivers’ phones is proof of the value of measuring driver performance for businesses.

How Sooffer is Using GPS

Sooffer’s service is built around their smartphone app used by both drivers and customers, which gives them an opportunity to collect GPS data during Sooffer trips. Data is constantly collected during trips and sent to Sooffer’s servers for processing and long-term storage. We have CMS (Content Management System) in place to collect all the data from drivers and customers.

 

Sooffer sends drivers reports on their driving habits, complaints and feedbacks given by the customers by analyzing the trip data. But also stores data to find long-term driver- or location-specific trends.

 

For example, Sooffer might terminate drivers contract off that they find to be consistently dangerous or poorly performing drivers, or Sooffer might store data for use in other services, such as the rate of hard braking in a certain city. This long-term data might be especially useful in Sooffer’s work in developing autonomous cars.
